Profile

Creative technologist, developer and educator working in generative and interactive media: real-time visual systems for installations, live performance and clinical environments, from concept through on-site commissioning. Practice runs on two tracks, visuals and systems: generative, environment-aware content built in TouchDesigner, ComfyUI and Three.js, and the sensing, display and software architecture that ties it to a space. Teaches creative technology at NYU and performs and exhibits in New York City as rgb__tears.
