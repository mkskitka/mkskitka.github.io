Profile

Creative technologist, developer and educator with over 8 years of experience building generative and interactive media, from initial ideation through deployment. Most recently built PGN, a multi-room interactive rehabilitation system that runs body and eye tracking, depth cameras and lidar through a single TouchDesigner system, now installed at clinical sites. Earlier work includes a real-time generative AI visuals system for an installation at the Venice Biennale '24, and four years building visualization tools for AI research. Has taught courses and workshops in creative technology at NYU.
